# E5 analysis (numbers: `results.md`, raw: `results/`)

## What the data says
- **Detection, not protocol, dominates.** With BFD, a gray failure costs about 0.75 s on OSPF and 0.81 s on eBGP (medians of impacted runs: 753 ms and 812 ms), i.e. roughly the 900 ms BFD detection time. Without BFD the same failure takes 7 s and 31 s in the two impacted OSPF runs (third run unaffected, N=3), 17 to 18 s on IS-IS (N=3) and 7.8 to 11.3 s on eBGP with a 9 s hold timer (N=10). The protocol changes the number by a small factor; BFD changes it by one to two orders of magnitude.
- **Carrier loss** (clean link down) is at the noise floor for eBGP and OSPF (medians 4 to 5 ms). **IS-IS** showed 2 s outages in 2 of 10 runs (1913 and 2051 ms), the only protocol with clean-failure outliers at that size; I did not investigate why (candidates: SPF/LSP timers, not checked).
- **IS-IS gray + BFD** came out at about 1.6 s (1546 to 1777 ms), roughly twice the other two, although BFD detects in under a second; the extra time is probably IS-IS's own SPF/flooding after BFD tears down the adjacency (not verified).
- **Config size** for one leaf: eBGP 52, OSPF + iBGP RR 63, IS-IS + iBGP RR 68 non-comment lines. The eBGP design needs the fewest lines because it is one protocol for underlay and overlay.

## Caveats, read before quoting
- **Runs that did not hit the flow.** In 1 of 10 (OSPF gray + BFD) and 4 of 10 (IS-IS gray + BFD) runs, no loss occurred or the outage was under 100 ms. Most likely the flow was not using the failed uplink at injection time (the IGP variants showed route/path changes between the pre-run "which uplink" measurement and the injection; not proven). Those runs are shown in the all-runs median but excluded from the "impacted" statistics. In E1 (eBGP) every gray run was hit.
- The no-BFD rows have only N=3 and very wide spread (OSPF 7 s to 31 s). OSPF's 7 s is far below its 40 s dead interval: a plausible reason is that iBGP sessions over the same path hit their 9 s hold timer first, which is a protocol-interaction effect, not OSPF detecting the fault. Not verified.
- One IS-IS carrier run lost 5719 packets with a 3 ms longest gap and another 1913 packets; the loss counts do not match the gaps, so these runs probably include a trailing outage or a receiver-side problem. They are in the raw data and not hidden; the aggregate uses the trailing-gap rule.
- In the **OSPF** variant, `fabricx validate` reported 3 findings (`experiments/E5/run.log`: no Type-5 for 10.30.30.0/24, the BLUE subnet, seen from leaf2 and leaf3) that the IS-IS variant did not. **Update (Phase 7):** the first GitHub CI runs showed the same symptom on the *eBGP* fabric, and the cause was a start-up race (FRR booted before `setup.sh` built the kernel VRF, so the L3VNI was bound to an automatically created BGP instance that never originated Type-5 routes). After the fix, a fresh OSPF deploy and a fresh IS-IS deploy both validated PASS (one deploy each, so consistent with that explanation, not a proof). The probe flow in E5 is the *bridged* path (Type-2/3 only), so the E5 timings were not affected by the missing routes.
- Software forwarding, shared CPUs and clock; same probe resolution limits as E1 (3 to 9 ms).
